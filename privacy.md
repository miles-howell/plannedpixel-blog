This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit **{{ site.title }}** (`blog.plannedpixel.com`, the "Site"), how it is used, and the choices you have. The Site is a personal blog run by Miles Howell. In short: **I don't sell your data, I don't run ads, and there are no accounts.** The only tracking is Google Analytics, which tells me roughly how many people read what.

## Who is responsible

The Site is operated by Miles Howell. If you have any questions or requests about your data, email [{{ site.social.email }}](mailto:{{ site.social.email }}).

## Information collected

### Analytics (Google Analytics)

The Site uses **Google Analytics 4** to see which posts get read and how visitors find them. When you load a page, Google Analytics may set cookies (such as `_ga` and `_ga_*`) and collect information such as:

- Pages viewed, time on page, and how you arrived (for example, a search engine or a link from another site)
- Approximate location (city/country level), derived from your IP address
- Device, browser, operating system, screen size, and language

Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. I use this data only in aggregate to understand readership; I don't try to identify individual visitors, and I don't use it for advertising, remarketing, or profiling. Analytics only runs on the published site, not on local previews.

Google processes this data under its own terms. See [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Comments (utterances / GitHub)

Post comments use [utterances](https://utteranc.es/), which stores comments as public issues in a GitHub repository. To comment, you sign in with GitHub and authorize the utterances app. Your comment, GitHub username, and avatar then become **publicly visible** on the Site and on GitHub. GitHub's [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) covers that data. The comment widget loads from utteranc.es and GitHub even if you only read and don't comment.

### Email

If you email me, I receive your email address and anything you include in the message. I use it only to reply, and I don't add you to any mailing list.

### Server logs

The Site is a static website hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. I don't have access to those logs. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Third-party resources

To render pages, the Site loads fonts and scripts from third-party content delivery networks, including Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`) and jsDelivr (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`). Those providers receive your IP address and standard browser information when the files are requested. Links to other websites, and the optional share buttons on posts (X/Twitter, Facebook, Telegram), are governed by those sites' own privacy policies. Share buttons are plain links and only contact those services if you click them.

## Cookies and local storage

| What | Set by | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` cookies | Google Analytics | Distinguish visitors and sessions for aggregate statistics |
| Browser session/local storage | The Site's theme | Remember your light/dark mode preference |
| Service worker cache | The Site | Lets the Site load faster and work offline (as an installable web app) |
| GitHub cookies | GitHub (only if you sign in to comment) | Authentication for commenting |

The theme storage and offline cache stay on your device and are never sent to me.

## Your choices

- **Block or delete cookies** using your browser settings. The Site works fine without them.
- **Opt out of Google Analytics** on all sites with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), or use a content or tracker blocker.
- **Global Privacy Control / Do Not Track:** the Site doesn't currently respond to these signals automatically. The tools above stop analytics if you want that.
- **Delete a comment** by deleting or editing it on GitHub, or by asking me to remove it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information. Because I hold very little data that can identify you, most requests are best handled directly with Google or GitHub. I'm still happy to help: email [{{ site.social.email }}](mailto:{{ site.social.email }}). Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for analytics is my legitimate interest in understanding how the Site is used. For comments and email, it's your consent and your request.

I don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Data retention

Google Analytics data is kept for Google Analytics' configured retention period (no longer than 14 months for detailed data) and is then deleted automatically. Comments stay on GitHub until you or I remove them. Emails are kept as long as needed to keep up the conversation.

## Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them.
